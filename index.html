to: Maha 01 / 05

Dear Maha,

I keep trying to find a sentence big enough for what you mean to me, and every sentence feels a little too small. So let me start with the simplest truth: loving you has made my life feel more like itself.

Not louder. Not perfect. Just more honest, more awake, and much more worth remembering.

The little things

I love the way you make ordinary moments glow without even trying. A look across the room. A laugh that arrives before the punchline. The calm that settles in when I know you are near.

You have this quiet magic, Maha. You turn “just another day” into a day I want to keep.

What you give me

You make me want to be braver with my heart. You remind me that tenderness is not something to hide, and that being understood can feel like coming home.

With you, I do not have to perform a better version of myself. I can simply arrive — and still be loved.

My promise

I cannot promise that every day will be easy. But I can promise to keep choosing the truth, to listen with my whole heart, and to notice the small beautiful things that make you, you.

I will meet you in the middle, in the messy parts, and in all the soft places we have not discovered yet.

The truest part

If I could give you one thing, I would give you a mirror that shows you the way I see you: rare, luminous, deeply loved, and more precious than you know.

And if love is a place, then Maha, you are the place my heart keeps finding.

Yours, in all the little ways,
always.
